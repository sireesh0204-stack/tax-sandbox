# Syllabus — Complex US Returns (Oct 2 → Nov 1, 2026)

Format: each module = **concept lesson → sandbox return (I role-play the client) → interview drill.**
Every return: you gather facts from me by asking client questions, I correct your questions and your entries, then you defend the return like a reviewer would.

| # | Topic | Sandbox scenario |
|---|---|---|

| **Gate** | Prepare a full 1065 + 3 K-1s unaided in the sandbox, then defend it | |
