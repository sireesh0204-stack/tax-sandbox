# Lesson 1 — HNI 1040 Intake Interview: Meet the Client

**Goal:** Practice the client-intake interview for a high-net-worth individual with multiple pass-through entities. You will ask me (the client) the questions a real senior preparer would ask, catch the contradictions I hide in my answers, and build a clean workpaper before entering numbers into the return.

---

## The Client's Opening Statement

> "Hi, I'm **David Vance**. I live in Austin, Texas. Last year was a big year for me.
> I have a W-2 from my day job as a tech VP, but most of my wealth is in real estate
> and two partnership investments.
>
> Here's what I think happened in 2025:
> 1. **Salary:** $380,000 W-2 from CloudScale Inc. in Austin (no state income tax in TX, nice and simple!).
> 2. **Real estate partnership (Vance-Miller Properties LLC, a 1065):** I own 40%. They gave me a K-1 showing $120,000 of ordinary rental income (Box 2) and a cash distribution of $150,000. I took the cash and bought a boat.
> 3. **Tech startup partnership (Apex AI Labs LP, another 1065):** I'm a limited partner, 15% share. The K-1 shows an ordinary business loss of ($45,000) in Box 1. I contributed $10,000 capital 3 years ago and haven't put anything in since.
> 4. **Direct rental property:** A single-family rental in San Jose, California that I manage myself. It made about $35,000 net after expenses, and I paid $4,200 in California state tax on it.
> 5. **Stock trading:** I sold some shares through Morgan Stanley and have a 1099-B showing $60,000 in long-term capital gains and $8,000 in short-term gains.
> 6. **Foreign stuff:** I keep about $85,000 in a savings account in Singapore from when I worked there in 2022. It earned around $2,100 in interest.
>
> My old CPA retired and gave me my 2024 return. What other questions do you have for me?"

---

## Your Turn — The Intake Interview

You are the preparer. **Do NOT start entering numbers yet.**

A junior preparer will say: "Great, send me your W-2, the two K-1s, and the 1099-B, and I'll do the return."
A **senior preparer** (the one who gets hired at 20+ LPA) will look at this statement and immediately spot **at least 5 red flags, missing facts, and hidden contradictions** that change the tax calculation completely.

### Your task right now:

Reply with the **list of questions** you are asking me (the client, David Vance). For each question:
1. **The question you'd ask me in plain English** (as you'd say it to a client), and
2. **The tax reason** why you need that answer (which form, limitation, or worksheet does it affect?).

I will answer in character, and I will grade your intake on whether you caught the hidden traps. Go ahead — interview me!
